- **The zero is guaranteed twice over.** Under the percentage rule the manufacturer-cost sign equals the effective-cost sign, and at log rho zero the accepted sample is exactly the nonnegative-effective-cost sample. "No failures on the accepted sample" could not have come out otherwise.
